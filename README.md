### 📘 My Cybersecurity Journey

My journey started with pure curiosity — understanding how systems break, behave, and evolve. Over time, that curiosity transformed into a structured path focused on **ethical hacking, red teaming, secure development**, and **threat analysis**.

I have consistently pushed myself to explore all layers of security:
- 🔍 **Low-level system behavior**
- 🌐 **Web exploitation & modern attack vectors**
- 🧩 **Capture The Flag problem-solving**
- 🛡️ **Defensive strategies and incident handling**

My long-term mission is to become a top-tier security engineer, capable of building and defending real-world systems at scale.
